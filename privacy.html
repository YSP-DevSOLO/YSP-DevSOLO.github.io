Petal Pots: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Petal Pots ("the game") is made by YSP ("we"). This page explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Questions: sopanhayim2001@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not create an account. We never ask for your name, email, photos or contacts.
  • Your progress, coins and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game is free because it shows ads. Our advertising and statistics partners (Google) collect some device information to do that.
  • The game is not directed at children under 13.

Information saved on your device

Your level, coins, hints, unlocked skins, daily streak, settings and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved on your device. This information is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Information collected by our partners

The game includes software from Google that collects information automatically:

Google explains how it uses this information here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Personalised ads and your choices

  • Consent form. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), the game asks for your choice before any ad is requested. You can change it at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • iPhone and iPad. The game asks Apple's permission question before using your advertising identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it is not used. You can change this in the Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in the Settings app under Privacy > Ads.
  • Saying no does not change the game. You see the same number of ads; they are just less tailored to you.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase removes banner and full-screen ads. Optional reward videos remain available if you choose to watch them.

Children

The game is for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help have it deleted.

How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete the game. Google keeps analytics data for a limited period (we use a retention setting of 14 months or less) and advertising data according to its own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account for you, the quickest routes are the in-game Privacy choices, your device's advertising settings, and deleting the game. You can also email us and we will help you make a request to Google. Residents of California and other US states have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising; use the in-game Privacy choices to do so.

Legal basis (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

We rely on your consent for personalised advertising and for statistics that require it, and on our legitimate interest in running, securing and improving the game for the rest.

International transfers

Google may process information on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the effective date above.
