Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what the FaceRoll iPhone app ("the app") does with information. FaceRoll is published by the FaceRoll team ("we", "us").

The short version: your selfies and cartoon avatars never leave your iPhone. We do not run accounts or servers that receive them. The app shows ads, and the advertising provider collects some device and usage data to do that.

1. Photos and cartoon avatars

  • The app asks for camera access so players can take a selfie. You can also pick a photo from your library.
  • Face detection, background removal and the cartoon effect all run on your iPhone. Photos are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The app locates faces only to crop and stylise the picture. It does not identify who anyone is and does not create face templates for recognition.
  • If "Save players" is on, player names, photos and avatars are stored in the app's private storage on your iPhone so they are there next time. Turning the setting off, using "Delete All Players", or deleting the app removes them.

2. Settings

Your choices in Settings (sound, vibration, theme and similar) are stored on your iPhone only.

3. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device identifiers, including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address, which indicates your general location;
  • device and app information, such as model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data.

Before any tracking, iOS asks whether you allow the app to track you. If you decline, ads are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your photos, avatars and player names are never shared with advertising providers.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not sell personal information.

5. Children

FaceRoll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Remove camera access in iPhone Settings > FaceRoll.
  • Delete saved players in the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Control ad tracking in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights to access or delete personal data. Because we hold no personal data about you on our own systems, requests about advertising data should go to Google using the links above; for anything else, contact us.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, we will update this page and its effective date.
