How do I add players?

Tap Add Players, type a name, pick a cartoon style and tap Snap Selfie. You need at least two players to roll.

The camera does not open

Open iPhone Settings > FaceRoll and turn on Camera. Then return to the app and try again.

I cannot hear any sound

Sounds follow the silent switch on the side of the iPhone. Also check that Sound effects is on in the app's Settings (the gear button).

Shaking the phone does nothing

Check that Shake to roll is on in the app's Settings.

What does "Remove winner after each roll" do?

Whoever is picked sits out until everyone has had a turn, then a new round starts. Tap Reset round to start over early.

How do I rename or remove a player?

Tap Edit Players. Type in the name field to rename; names save as you type. Swipe a player to the left to remove them.

My players disappeared

Players are kept between launches only when Save players is on in Settings. Turning it off deletes the saved copy.

How do I delete my photos?

Open Settings in the app and tap Delete All Players, or delete the app. Photos are stored only on your iPhone; see the privacy policy.
